About Our Weekly Maintenance Service

🏠

Consistent Care

Regular maintenance keeps your home consistently fresh, healthy, and welcoming. We take care of the routine so you can enjoy the results every week.

⏱️

Efficient Service

Our team works efficiently within 2-3 hour visits to maintain your home's cleanliness and keep everything looking its best.

✦

Rotating Detail Cleaning

Each week, we focus on specific detail areas on a rotating basis—so every part of your home gets the attention it deserves over time.

🎯

Maintenance, Not Deep Cleaning

We focus on maintaining the cleanliness of your home, not performing heavy restoration. This keeps your space consistently polished.

What's Included

In Our Weekly Maintenance Service

Kitchen

Wipe countertops & backsplash • Clean sink & polish fixtures • Wipe cabinet fronts • Clean appliance exteriors • Empty trash & replace liner • Sweep & mop floors

Bedrooms

Make beds & straighten bedding • Dust all surfaces & décor • Wipe nightstands & dressers • Straighten & tidy • Vacuum floors • Empty trash & replace liner

Bathrooms

Clean & sanitize countertops • Clean sink & polish fixtures • Clean mirrors • Clean toilet inside & out • Wipe cabinets • Sweep & mop floors

Living Areas

Throughout Home

Extras We Love

Change bed linens • Fold & put away laundry • Organize small areas • Refresh high-traffic spots • Just ask about additional services we offer!
