How It Works

What to Expect

✦

No surprises — here is exactly how weekly service runs, from the first visit onward.

  1. 1

    We learn your home

    Before the first visit you'll complete a short welcome packet: your cleaning priorities room by room, any areas you'd rather we skip, fragile or sentimental items, pets, and any allergies or scent sensitivities we should work around.

  2. 2

    You choose a day and an arrival window

    Your monthly price reserves a recurring cleaning day that stays yours. Arrival windows are 8:00–11:00 AM, 11:00 AM–2:00 PM, 2:00–5:00 PM, or flexible. Windows are approximate, and we'll let you know if anything meaningfully affects your scheduled time.

  3. 3

    We agree how we get in

    Most clients provide a key, lockbox, garage code, or door code, along with alarm instructions and any doors or areas that shouldn't be entered. Access details stay private and are used only to complete your scheduled service. Pets that might be at risk underfoot should be secured.

  4. 4

    We bring the products — you keep the equipment

    We bring all cleaning products, microfiber cloths, scrub brushes and pads, dusters, detail tools, and standard disposables. To prevent cross-contamination between homes, each client keeps their own vacuum, broom, mop, and toilet brush on hand.

  5. 5

    We clean, then rotate the details

    Every visit covers the weekly checklist across your kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, and living areas in about two to three hours. On top of that we rotate through detail work — baseboards, ceiling fans, interior windows, vents, cabinet fronts — so every part of your home gets attention over time.

  6. 6

    You're invoiced once a month

    Invoices go out monthly through Square or PayPal to your preferred email or phone number, due on the 1st. Pricing is a flat monthly rate that reserves your recurring appointment — if a month happens to contain five scheduled visits, there's no extra charge.

Going away? Recurring service can be paused for up to two weeks per calendar year with at least 14 days' notice, and your reserved day stays yours.

Common Questions

Questions, Answered

✦
What areas do you serve?

We serve Russellville, Littleville, Tuscumbia, Muscle Shoals, and the surrounding areas of northwest Alabama. If you're just outside those towns, call or text (256) 257-2394 and we'll let you know.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Most clients provide a key, lockbox, garage code, or door code, along with alarm instructions and a note of any doors or areas that shouldn't be entered. Those access details are kept private and used only to complete your scheduled service.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

We bring all cleaning products, microfiber cloths, scrub brushes and pads, dusters, detail tools, and standard disposable supplies. Each client provides their own vacuum, broom, mop, and toilet brush, which we use only in that home — this prevents cross-contamination between houses. Just keep them accessible and in working order.

Is this a deep clean?

No. This is a weekly maintenance service designed for upkeep, not deep cleaning or heavy restoration. Every visit completes the standard checklist, and detail tasks rotate over time so areas like baseboards, ceiling fans, interior windows, and vents each get proper attention without extending your regular visit.

How long does a visit take?

Most visits run about two to three hours. Time and scope depend on the size and condition of the home — a well-maintained home may be fully completed each visit, while others receive rotating focus areas.

How much does weekly cleaning cost?

Weekly maintenance starts at $400 per month, which covers four weekly visits of two to three hours on a reserved cleaning day, with five-week months included at no extra charge. The starting tier suits well-maintained, single-level homes of up to three bedrooms and two bathrooms. Larger homes, multi-level homes, and homes needing more weekly time receive a personalised quote. Deep cleans are quoted individually, and estimates are free.

Do you offer deep cleaning?

Yes, as a separate service from weekly maintenance. A lived-in deep clean is a detailed reset for a home you're living in, covering high dust and cobwebs, detailed kitchen and bathrooms, ceiling fans and light fixtures, doors, frames, trim and baseboards, and hard-to-reach floor areas. A move-in or move-out deep clean covers a complete empty home including inside cabinets and drawers, closets, appliance interiors, and interior windows and tracks. Both are quoted individually.

How does billing work?

Invoices are sent monthly through Square or PayPal to your preferred email address or phone number, and payment is due on the 1st of the month. We accept Square invoice, PayPal, and cash. Your monthly price is a flat rate that reserves your recurring appointment, so a month with five scheduled visits costs no more than one with four.

Can I pause service while I'm away?

Yes. Recurring service can be paused for up to two weeks per calendar year with at least 14 days' notice, and your reserved cleaning day stays yours. Longer holds may mean the reserved appointment can't be kept open.

Do I need to tidy up before you arrive?

A quick pick-up helps. Excessive clutter reduces how much cleaning we can complete in the scheduled time, so clear paths and picked-up toys, clothing, and loose items mean more of your visit goes to actual cleaning. Running water and electricity also need to be available during service.

What about pets?

Pets are welcome to be home. We just ask that any pet who might interfere with the work or create an unsafe situation is secured before we arrive, and that you share their names and any handling instructions in advance.

Is there anything you don't clean?

Biohazards, excessive mold, pest infestations, human or animal waste beyond normal household cleaning, and any unsafe condition fall outside the scope of this service. If we encounter one, we'll stop and talk with you before continuing. Please also let us know in advance about fragile, valuable, or sentimental items that need special care.
