The basics we handle each week
01

Kitchen

  • Wipe countertops & backsplash
  • Clean sink & polish fixtures
  • Wipe cabinet fronts
  • Clean appliance exteriors
  • Empty trash & replace liner
  • Sweep & mop floors
02

Bedrooms

  • Make beds & straighten bedding
  • Dust all surfaces & décor
  • Wipe nightstands & dressers
  • Straighten & tidy
  • Vacuum floors
  • Empty trash & replace liner
03

Bathrooms

  • Clean & sanitize countertops
  • Clean sink & polish fixtures
  • Clean mirrors
  • Clean toilet inside & out
  • Wipe cabinets
  • Sweep & mop floors
04

Living Areas

  • Dust all surfaces & décor
  • Wipe tables & shelves
  • Fluff pillows & straighten
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Sweep hard floors
  • Empty trash & replace liner
05

Throughout the Home

  • Dust all reachable surfaces
  • Wipe light switches & door knobs
  • Straighten & tidy spaces
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Sweep hard floors
  • Empty trash & replace liners
06

Extras We Love

  • Change bed linens
  • Fold & put away laundry
  • Organize small areas
  • Refresh high-traffic spots
