About Our Service

Weekly Maintenance, Done Right

🏠

Consistent Care

Regular maintenance keeps your home consistently fresh, healthy, and welcoming — every single week.

⏱

2–3 Hour Visits

Our team works efficiently to maintain your home's cleanliness without disrupting your day.

✦

Rotating Detail Cleaning

Each visit, we focus on specific detail areas on rotation — so every part of your home gets attention over time.

🎯

Maintenance, Not Deep Cleaning

We focus on upkeep, not heavy restoration — keeping your home consistently polished.

What's Included

Every Visit, Every Detail

01

Kitchen

Wipe countertops & backsplash, clean sink & polish fixtures, wipe cabinet fronts, clean appliance exteriors, empty trash, sweep & mop floors.

02

Bedrooms

Make beds & straighten bedding, dust surfaces & décor, wipe nightstands & dressers, vacuum floors, empty trash.

03

Bathrooms

Sanitize countertops, clean sink & polish fixtures, clean mirrors, clean toilet inside & out, sweep & mop floors.

04

Living Areas

Dust surfaces & décor, wipe tables & shelves, fluff pillows, vacuum carpets & rugs, empty trash.

05

Throughout Home

Dust reachable surfaces, wipe switches & door knobs, vacuum carpets & rugs, sweep hard floors, empty liners.

06

Extras We Love

Change bed linens, fold & put away laundry, organize small areas, refresh high-traffic spots. Just ask!
