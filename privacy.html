Your Information

Privacy Policy

✦

Last updated 28 July 2026

The Polished House is a residential cleaning service based in Tuscumbia, Alabama. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it — both through this website and in the course of cleaning your home.

Information collected through this website

The quote request form on this site asks for your name, phone number, and a description of your home. Email address and service address are optional. We collect this only to respond to your enquiry and prepare an estimate.

Form submissions are delivered to us by Formspree, a third-party form service, which processes and forwards the message to our email inbox. Formspree handles that data under its own privacy policy. We do not use the information you submit for anything other than replying to you.

This site sets no advertising cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and does not build a profile of your visit. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the form script is loaded from unpkg, so those providers may see your IP address as part of serving those files.

Information collected as a client

Clients complete a welcome packet before service begins. Depending on what you choose to share, this may include your name, service address, phone numbers, email addresses, billing details, preferred entry method and entry instructions, alarm information, pet names and handling instructions, cleaning priorities, areas not to clean, and any fragile or valuable items needing special care.

We collect this information for one reason: to provide the service you have asked for, safely and to your preferences.

Access and security details

Entry codes, lockbox information, alarm instructions, and keys are treated as confidential. They are used only to complete your scheduled service, are not shared outside the business, and are not published or stored anywhere publicly accessible.

Payment information

Invoices are issued through Square or PayPal. Card and bank details are entered with and held by those providers, under their own privacy and security policies. We do not collect or store your card numbers.

Photographs

We may photograph cleaned areas to showcase our work. No people, personal documents, addresses, or identifying information will intentionally appear in these photographs. Clients indicate on the welcome packet whether they authorise before-and-after photographs for advertising and social media. If you do not authorise it, we will not publish photographs of your home. You may change that choice at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the business — Formspree for website enquiries, Square and PayPal for invoicing and payment — or where we are required to do so by law.

Retention

Client records are kept for as long as you are a client and for a reasonable period afterwards for billing and tax purposes. Enquiries that do not become bookings are kept only as long as needed to follow up.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons. You may withdraw photograph authorisation at any time. You may ask us to stop contacting you.

To make any of these requests, call or text (256) 257-2394, or email ThePolishedHouseCompany@gmail.com. We will respond as promptly as we reasonably can.
